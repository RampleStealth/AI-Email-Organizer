## Historical context

This file records that an early Wong Studio constitution draft was created in the AI Email Organizer repository before Wong Studio established its canonical company repository.

The draft never became approved authority. Its former principles, hierarchy, governance language, and amendment language have been removed from this product repository so they cannot compete with the approved company sources.

## Current authority

Constitutional authority resides exclusively in the [Wong Studio repository](https://github.com/RampleStealth/Wong-Studio/tree/v1.0.0). The applicable authority is the approved [Wong Studio Constitution v1](https://github.com/RampleStealth/Wong-Studio/blob/v1.0.0/docs/01-constitution/constitution-v1.md), together with its [Repository Policy](https://github.com/RampleStealth/Wong-Studio/blob/v1.0.0/docs/01-constitution/repository-policy.md) and [Governance](https://github.com/RampleStealth/Wong-Studio/blob/v1.0.0/docs/01-constitution/governance.md).

This historical record grants no constitutional, governance, cultural, design, language, engineering, AI, or trust authority to AI Email Organizer. Product-specific authority remains in the product documentation.
